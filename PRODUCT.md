# FleshCuts LLC — product

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS, no frameworks, no tracking; hosted on GitHub Pages
- **Surface mode:** Persuade (one-page site for a local service business)

## Users
- **Primary:** homeowners in Brick, NJ and surrounding communities who need a fall cleanup (leaves, brush, overgrown beds) or want snow shoveling booked before winter. They find the link on Facebook, on a phone, inside Facebook's in-app browser. (Inferred from the Facebook-group ad the business was found through.)
- **First reader:** Brandon, the owner. The site is a free gift meant to win him over; he should recognise his own business within one phone screen.

## Purpose
Turn a single Facebook-group ad into a real home on the web: show what he does, show his real cleanup results, and make it one tap to text him for a free estimate.

## Positioning
A neighbor, not a company. One person (Brandon) with 8+ years of landscaping experience who works full time in grounds and maintenance for Brick Public Schools, and does fall cleanups and snow shoveling for neighbors. Free estimates, competitive prices, price matching.

## Capabilities and constraints
- Contact is call or text to (570) 690-1250 (flyer: "Call or text"). His own intake: "Call or text … with your town and a few photos".
- No email, no booking system, no reviews, no published prices, no Google listing.
- Phone area code 570 is northeastern PA while the business is in Brick NJ — used as given, flagged for the owner to confirm.
- Licensing/insurance appears on his flyer but the brief says not to publish it until verified.

## Brand commitments
- Name: **FleshCuts LLC** (logo reads "FleshCuts — complete land care — EST 2019").
- Logo: hand-drawn crayon circle — Brandon pushing a mower, a dachshund in a propeller cap carrying a flower, blue sky, green grass. Source: Facebook profile picture.
- Flyer palette (his own ad): deep forest green, warm cream ground, soft beige and pale blue panels.
- Tone: neighborly and direct, first person — "Hi neighbors, I'm Brandon, owner of FleshCuts LLC in Brick."

## Evidence on hand
- Two before/after cleanup pairs (from his ad flyer, 1631×2048 original).
- Logo (1254px).
- Services verbatim: leaf & brush removal; garden beds & overgrowth; seasonal property cleanups; walkways & sidewalks; steps & entryways; driveways.

## Principles
1. Phone first; the first phone screen shows who, what, and the text action.
2. Only facts from the brief, the ad and the logo. Nothing invented.
3. Speak as Brandon, about how he treats a neighbor's yard.
4. The page lets you do the job: rake the leaves yourself.
